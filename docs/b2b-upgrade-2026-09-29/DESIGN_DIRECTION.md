# LZZ design direction for this upgrade

This supplements the existing `DESIGN.md` and does not replace its historical decisions. Reading this as an English industrial B2B site for overseas engineers and sourcing teams buying custom CNC parts from the actual LZZ operation. The intended visual language is restrained industrial editorial: real machining and part imagery lead, accurate buyer decisions follow, then complete technical detail and an honest mail-draft inquiry path.

- **Identity and type:** retain the LZZ mark, Inter Variable for headings and body, approximately 600–620 heading weight, readable 15–17 px body; do not apply generic display-serif or oversized Awwwards type.
- **Color and surfaces:** current charcoal `#282c2f`, action red `#cb1217`, white and neutral `#f8f8f7`, thin gray separators `#d8dcde`. No gradients, glassmorphism, gratuitous depth or new accent palette. Red marks the primary RFQ action, not every heading or decoration.
- **Grid and details:** current 1140 px main container, 40 px desktop gutter and compact mobile gutters; use editorial splits for image/evidence, readable single-column long-form content, 4–8 px control radii and restrained borders. Spacing serves groups and reading order, not dramatic emptiness.
- **Images:** product shapes and relevant process scenes appear early, but the first useful answer may precede a decorative illustration. Real LZZ photographs remain traceable proof; generated visuals are illustrative only and disclosed. Preserve part geometry and do not imply a customer order, measured result, certification, or actual facility where none is evidenced.
- **CTA and mobile:** the site prepares a buyer's email draft; labels must not imply a completed upload or send. Keep one clear primary action per decision point, preserve direct contact, and show all service choices at 390 px. On mobile, reorder comparisons and summaries for comprehension rather than just stacking columns.
- **Content contracts:** preserve 35 product routes, category and case pages, useful specifications, original evidence, canonical/SEO semantics, quality limitations, contact information and keyboard behavior. No new certificate, tolerance, factory-scale or delivery claim without source.

Skill division: `design-taste-frontend` (internal name `tasteskill`) informs contextual anti-template composition and the B2B design read; use only compatible principles. `impeccable` guides distillation, responsive organization, hierarchy and accessibility via its local references; its binary context loader is unavailable in this sandbox, so use the actual project `DESIGN.md` and screenshots directly. `apple-design` contributes predictable feedback, optical typography and spatial consistency only. Discard `gpt-taste`'s mandatory font change, random layout, huge whitespace and GSAP because these conflict with the user's established Inter industrial site and procurement task.
