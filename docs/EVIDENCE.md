# LZZ source-media evidence

`source-evidence-inventory.json` records every raster image and video in `D:/lzz/原始资料`: its relative source path, SHA-256, dimensions where readable, publication status and duplicate relationship by hash. `src/data/evidence.generated.json` contains the 22 hand-selected original photographs used by the site, with public captions and responsive WebP paths. `src/data/evidence.ts` is the page interface. Run `node scripts/build-evidence.mjs` after an approved media change; the source folder is read only.

All factory, inspection, process and shipment photographs newly published in this change are source photographs, not AI-generated evidence. The shipment image is cropped to remove paper labels; `publicationCrop` records the coordinates. The original remains unchanged. The older workshop and shipment photographs are labeled as such; folder dates are identified as folder dates rather than asserted capture dates. The park exterior is not published because unrelated tenant signs dominate the supplied photograph and a crop would obscure the shared-park context. Third-party industry images and images marked as AI-generated were excluded from factory/product proof.

The three process studies draw from the separately labeled five-axis, turning-then-milling and two-milling-setup source folders. They document visible operations and part features. They do not prove a specific alloy, dimension, tolerance, customer, certification or delivery result. Product galleries remain refined catalog presentations and do not include original-photo thumbnails.

The process capability heroes now use process-specific LZZ source photographs. The turning photograph was switched to the 4000×3000 source frame from the same group. Material presentation uses an original aluminum-housing batch photograph and two clearly labeled AI-retouched visuals derived from LZZ source photographs. The brass and polymer generated images are **illustrative presentations**, not documentary proof of specific dimensions, grade or a catalog SKU. Their source and output hashes, prompt constraints and web derivatives are recorded in `generated-visuals.json`; original PNG outputs are under `docs/media/generated/`. The aluminum display-housing panel and in-machine photograph, brass batch and turned-parts photographs remain separate original-source evidence lower on relevant capability pages. The quality page pairs the CMM room and CMM detail photographs, without presenting them as measurement reports or calibration records.

The About page now uses three further source photographs in a secondary people/archive section: a workshop visit, a Hangzhou event and a team gathering. Captions do not assert visitor identity, a contract, event endorsement or performance. Archived third-party logos are kept behind an expandable disclosure; their former presence on the LZZ site is not treated as verification of a present customer relationship. The product directory has an AI-retouched editorial image based on one text-heavy Alibaba cover. Two newly generated application-context images on About are explicitly labeled as illustrations. `editorial-visuals.json` records their prompts, provenance and hashes; `node scripts/build-editorial-visuals.mjs` rebuilds their WebP derivatives. None is used as factory evidence or an individual SKU gallery image.

`qa/video-review.json` and `qa/video-contact-sheet.webp` document visual screening of all seven videos at three points each. None is published in this release: the 29-inch housing clip includes an identifiable person and is vertical handheld footage, the PEEK clip is only 544×960 and soft, and the remaining clips do not improve the core three case presentations. A future use should be reviewed against the original clip, not inferred from folder names.

Current ISO9001 and RoHS/REACH documents were not present in the supplied source directory. The site retains the existing limited certificate disclosure; no certificate number, validity period or certificate image has been invented. When current documents arrive, verify legal entity, validity and scope before changing the public quality page.

`qa/evidence-qa.json` records the 390, 768 and 1440px browser checks, source-hash checks and screenshots for the home, factory, quality and case pages. It is regenerated with `TEST_URL=http://127.0.0.1:4323 node scripts/verify-evidence.mjs` when a local preview is running.
