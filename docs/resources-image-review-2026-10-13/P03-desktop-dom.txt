    - region "Drawing and quotation":
      - heading "Drawing and quotation" [level=2]
      - heading "1. Define part requirements" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Send your drawing or model with its revision and the material grade. Include the quantity and required delivery date, and describe the part function.
      - paragraph: If you are working from a sample, explain the fit and function to retain or change. Identify any material changes, then agree the measurement and drawing work needed to define the part.
      - heading "2. Review the quotation" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Check that the quotation uses the correct drawing revision. Discuss tool access and workholding with the team, and approve any proposed geometry changes against the mating features and critical dimensions.
      - link "Prepare the drawing and order inputs":
        - /url: /resources/rfq-checklist/
      - heading "Quotation scope" [level=3]
      - term: Machining & material
      - definition: Drawing revision, grade, quantity, price and any agreed alternative.
      - term: Surface finishing
      - definition: Included treatments and the surfaces to be controlled.
      - term: Inspection
      - definition: Report scope, sampling and required records.
      - term: Separate charges
      - definition: Tooling, special documentation, freight and duties where applicable.
      - term: Delivery terms
      - definition: Production timing, shipping arrangements and responsibilities.
      - heading "Payment and sample charges" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Before accepting a quote or placing an order, confirm the payment method and currency. Agree the payment stages and the amount due at each stage.
      - paragraph: For sample work, ask about machining and setup charges. Confirm sample freight and whether any sample fee can be credited to a later order.
    - region "Approve before production":
      - heading "Approve before production" [level=2]
      - heading "3. Check and approve the sample" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Set the sample acceptance criteria before machining. Identify critical interfaces and appearance requirements, with any functional tests needed.
      - paragraph: Set the first-article review stage and release criteria. Record the drawing revision and approval criteria before moving to production.
      - link "Release the specification for repeat orders":
        - /url: /resources/prototype-vs-small-batch-cnc/
      - heading "4. Agree on batch inspection" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Confirm the approved revision and checks for repeat production. Identify the features that need recorded results.
      - paragraph: Agree material traceability requirements and the treatment of nonconforming parts. Our quality planning guide covers the records to request.
      - link "View quality planning guide":
        - /url: /quality/
    - region "Packing and delivery documents":
      - heading "Packing and delivery documents" [level=2]
      - figure "Protected machined parts · LZZ packing archive, November 2022":
        - 'link "Open original archive image: Protected yellow and white machined components in a wooden packing case"':
          - /url: /media/evidence/shipping-example-1600.webp
          - img "Protected yellow and white machined components in a wooden packing case"
        - generic: Protected machined parts · LZZ packing archive, November 2022
      - heading "5. Arrange packing and shipment" [level=3]
      - paragraph: The parts and shipment requirements determine whether cartons or wooden cases are used. Identify polished faces and sealing surfaces that need protection, along with vulnerable threads.
      - paragraph: Specify the labels and part numbers required. Include the drawing revision and quantity per pack where needed. Provide the delivery address and receiving contact.
      - paragraph: Separate the required arrival date from the dispatch date. Confirm the shipping method, Incoterm, packing protection, transport costs and responsibility for customs, duties and taxes in the quotation. Any arrival-date commitment must also be agreed.
      - heading "6. Confirm documents for delivery" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Use the approved drawing revision and order reference consistently for packing, shipment and inspection documents.
      - paragraph: Specify the records needed with delivery, such as an agreed dimensional report or material documentation.
      - link "Packing archive and delivery checklist":
        - /url: /resources/custom-cnc-parts-shipping-checklist/
    - region "Report a nonconforming part":
      - heading "Report a nonconforming part" [level=2]
      - paragraph: Send the drawing revision and part or batch reference. Include the affected quantity, photographs and measurement details. We check the discrepancy against the order's acceptance criteria and discuss corrective action with you.
      - heading "After-sales terms" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Confirm any warranty period and its start date, exclusions and the procedure for reporting a problem.
      - paragraph: Agree responsibility for rework or replacement, return freight and any refund before ordering.
