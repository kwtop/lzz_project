    - heading "Identify the working surfaces" [level=2]
    - paragraph: A housing or shaft is located by the surfaces that meet other parts. Mark those interfaces on the drawing, including surfaces used to seal, slide or carry load. For a mounting block, identify the locating faces so tolerances can be assessed against the assembly function.

    - heading "Specify critical dimensions" [level=2]
    - paragraph: Give critical features their datums and acceptance limits. A 3D model defines shape, but a controlled 2D drawing may also be needed to specify fits and surface texture. Include edge conditions and measurement requirements where they affect acceptance.
    - paragraph:
      - text: Tolerances are assessed for each drawing. Achievable limits depend on the material and feature geometry, together with the setup and measurement method. Agree on

    - heading "Specify material and surface requirements" [level=2]
    - paragraph: State the exact alloy or plastic grade, material condition and any material-certificate requirement. For coated parts, specify the finish and whether critical dimensions apply before or after treatment. Mark areas that need masking, cosmetic faces and burr-sensitive edges.
    - paragraph:
      - text: Name the applicable standards and their revisions. Include traceability and approval requirements, with the intended end use and any export requirements. The
      - link "material and finish checklist":
        - /url: /resources/material-and-finish-checklist/
      - text: covers the detailed fields.
    - heading "Define acceptance checks and revisions" [level=2]
    - paragraph: Define the checks needed to accept the part, whether they concern assembly fit, appearance or a functional test. Set the criteria before machining. For a design change, issue a revised drawing identifying the affected features.
    - paragraph:
      - text: Record the controlled drawing revision, material, finish and approval criteria. Sample approval does not cover a changed design or material.
