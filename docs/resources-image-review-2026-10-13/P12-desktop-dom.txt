    - region "Packing and delivery requirements":
      - heading "Packing and delivery requirements" [level=2]
      - generic: Topic
      - generic: Information you provide
      - generic: Confirm in the quotation
      - list:
        - heading "Part protection" [level=3]
        - generic: Information you provide
        - paragraph: Mark cosmetic faces and sealing surfaces, then identify threads and fragile edges that need protection. State cleanliness and corrosion-protection needs. Specify pack weight or quantity limits and receiving-site handling requirements.
        - generic: Confirm in the quotation
        - paragraph: Agree protective materials, separation between parts and protection for threads and mating surfaces. Cartons or wooden cases depend on the part, quantity and transport route.
        - heading "Pack identification" [level=3]
        - generic: Information you provide
        - paragraph: Part number, drawing revision, quantity per pack, batch identification and required labels.
        - generic: Confirm in the quotation
        - paragraph: Confirm available label, lot-reference and inspection-document formats. Use the same part and revision references on the packing list and agreed quality records.
        - heading "Delivery basis" [level=3]
        - generic: Information you provide
        - paragraph: Full delivery address, receiving contact, required arrival date, requested trade term/version and named place.
        - generic: Confirm in the quotation
        - paragraph: Confirm who books transport and handles export/import clearance, insurance, duties, taxes and local delivery. EXW, FOB or DDP options are quoted for the applicable destination and service.
        - paragraph:
          - strong: DDP applies only where included in the quotation.
        - heading "Documents" [level=3]
        - generic: Information you provide
        - paragraph: The document list required by procurement, logistics or your destination-side import contact.
        - generic: Confirm in the quotation
        - paragraph: We provide protective packing with a packing list and commercial invoice, plus shipment information. Material and finishing records accompany the parts when specified in the order; the same applies to agreed dimensional records. Confirm which documents are available and who prepares each.
        - heading "Schedule" [level=3]
        - generic: Information you provide
        - paragraph: Separate your required arrival date from the desired dispatch date.
        - generic: Confirm in the quotation
        - paragraph: Production takes 7-14 business days after drawing and order confirmation, including surface finishing. Transit is estimated separately and is not included in production time.
    - heading "Transport options and import documents" [level=2]
    - paragraph: Courier, air or sea arrangements depend on shipment size and destination. DHL, FedEx or UPS can be discussed when selecting a courier.
    - paragraph: Agree additional customs documents before production with your importing entity or freight forwarder. Requirements depend on the destination and transaction.
