    - heading "Choosing CNC milling, turning or 5-axis machining" [level=1]
    - paragraph: A shaft or sleeve will usually start with turning. A housing with pockets and flat mounting faces is more likely to need milling. Angled features or access to several faces may call for a multi-axis setup. Some parts need a combination; send the drawing so we can review the sequence.

    - paragraph: Some turned parts also need milling, for example to add flats or cross-holes. A housing may require another setup to reach an angled face. The machining sequence must preserve the relationships between critical features, so send both a 3D model and the controlling 2D drawing.
    - heading "Accuracy depends on the feature" [level=2]
    - paragraph: Accuracy is assessed at the feature level. Geometry and material affect the achievable limit, together with the setup and datum strategy. Finishing and the measurement method also affect acceptance. State the tolerance and datum for each critical interface so its machining and inspection can be planned.
