    - heading "Validate the first sample" [level=2]
    - paragraph: Define the sample checks before machining. Assembly fit and critical interfaces may be the main concerns; appearance or operation may also need approval. Record the acceptance criteria before releasing a repeat order.
    - term: Drawing questions
    - definition: Use the sample to identify design questions related to fit, function or appearance.
    - term: Workholding and access
    - definition: Review workholding and tool access while confirming the design.
    - term: Material, finish and acceptance
    - definition: Check the specified grade, condition and final appearance. Define fit, function and sample acceptance checks. Confirm the prototype price and schedule for the specific part.
    - link "Prototype machining":

    - heading "Approve the repeat-order specification" [level=2]
    - paragraph: For a repeat order, release one controlled drawing revision and carry the approved material and finish into the quotation. Agree the acceptance plan and the documents to accompany delivery.
    - heading "Specification to carry forward" [level=3]
    - list:
      - listitem: Agree revision approval and how changed features are identified.
      - listitem: Confirm material documents and the finish standard, including masking and dimensions after treatment.
      - listitem: Retain the approved specification unless a change is agreed.
    - heading "Inspection records to agree" [level=3]
    - list:
      - listitem: Define batch sampling and critical-feature records.
      - listitem: Confirm the measurement method, instrument evidence and available report format.
      - listitem: Agree the acceptance plan and delivery documents.
    - paragraph: A drawing change may affect the approved sample. Confirm the changes before releasing the revised part.

    - heading "Plan the repeat order" [level=2]
    - paragraph: Confirm the setup and machining sequence for the released revision, including critical datums, and whether the route changes with quantity. Request a new quote for the batch quantity and price, and confirm the production slot.

      - strong: The economical batch size depends on the part and its setup.
      - text: Quantities are assessed for each drawing.

    - definition: 1 piece
    - term: Production lead time
    - definition: 7-14 business days
    - paragraph: After drawing and order confirmation, including surface finishing. Shipping time is additional.
    - paragraph: Share the part, quantity and required arrival date so we can quote price and delivery arrangements.

    - paragraph: Make sure each quote uses the same drawing revision, material and finish.
    - heading "Quoted scope" [level=3]
    - paragraph: Check setup, inspection records, packaging and delivery basis are stated.
