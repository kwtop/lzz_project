      - heading "Define how the part will be accepted" [level=2]
      - heading "Send a consistent drawing package" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Include a 3D model, such as STEP, and a 2D drawing when dimensions, fits or surface requirements are not fully defined in the model. Label the part number, units and drawing revision. If the files disagree, identify which document controls before requesting a quotation.
      - paragraph: For multi-axis parts, include the overall envelope, stock dimensions and part weight where known so tool access and setup can be reviewed.
      - complementary:
        - heading "Only have a sample?" [level=4]
        - paragraph: For an existing sample without a drawing, explain what is known and ask what additional measurement or design work would be needed. A sample alone cannot establish hidden material or treatment requirements.
      - heading "Mark the dimensions used for acceptance" [level=3]
      - paragraph: Identify mating bores and shaft fits on the drawing, with their datums and tolerances. Mark sealing faces, threads and any other critical features too. State whether dimensions apply before or after finishing.
      - paragraph: If you need a particular inspection record, send the format or an example with your inquiry. Agree on inspection and documentation requirements before ordering, and ask LZZ to confirm the measurement method, instrument range, calibration evidence and availability of any project-specific records required for your part.
