    - heading "Drawing & acceptance" [level=2]
    - heading "Drawing requirements" [level=3]
    - paragraph: Identify the assembly datums and dimensions that locate mating bores, mounting holes and faces. Separate functional dimensions from general dimensions.
    - paragraph: Mark deep pockets and small internal corners, along with thin sections that need tool-access or workholding checks. State where a radius or geometry change is acceptable.
    - heading "Inspection and acceptance" [level=3]
    - paragraph: Identify which face, bore and hole-position relationships need recorded results, and the datum system used to evaluate them.
    - paragraph: Specify whether dimensional acceptance applies before or after finishing, with masking and cosmetic surfaces marked on the drawing.
    - paragraph: Agree the measurement method and required records before production.
    - paragraph:
      - strong: ±0.01 mm — Evaluated critical-feature reference.
      - text: Selected critical features may be evaluated against this reference after engineering review. Material, geometry, setup and measurement method affect feasibility. It is not a default or guaranteed tolerance.
