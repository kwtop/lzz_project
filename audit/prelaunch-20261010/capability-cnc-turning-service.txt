    - term: Geometry & process
    - definition: For shafts, sleeves, bushings and fittings built around a rotational axis. Stepped diameters, bores, grooves and threads can be combined in turned components. Live tooling adds cross-holes, flats, slots and milled details to cylindrical parts. Fitting services include NPT, BSPT and metric threads; state the thread standard, mating component and sealing requirements.
    - term: Size & workholding
    - definition: Send the model, stock size, overall diameter and axial lengths. Identify the functional axis, shoulders, internal grooves and off-axis features so tool access and clamping space can be reviewed.
    - term: Material & finish
    - definition: State the stock grade, condition and surface finish on the drawing. Identify sealing and sliding surfaces, mating-part requirements and the edges that need deburring.
