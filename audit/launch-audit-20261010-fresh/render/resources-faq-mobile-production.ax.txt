				87 heading Can every feature hold ±0.01 mm?, Value: 3
					88 text Can every feature hold ±0.01 mm?
				89 text No. ±0.01 mm is a conditional engineering reference for selected critical features after review, not a general part tolerance or guarantee. Material, geometry, setup and measurement method determine whether it applies. Identify each critical feature on the drawing for confirmation.
