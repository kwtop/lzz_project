			135 heading Define the finish requirements, Value: 2, ID: section-7
				136 text Define the finish requirements
			137 text Specify visual appearance separately from the finish's function. Name the required finish standard and revision where applicable, and describe color, gloss, texture, coating thickness or roughness only where these are controlled requirements. Mark masked areas, cosmetic faces and surfaces for corrosion protection, electrical contact or sealing. Confirm process suitability, availability and required documentation for the selected material and part.
			138 text A photograph shows surface appearance but cannot establish coating thickness or surface roughness. It cannot verify process compliance either, so a reference image alone is not a finish specification.
