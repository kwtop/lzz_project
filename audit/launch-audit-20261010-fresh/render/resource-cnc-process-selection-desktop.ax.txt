			77 heading When does a part need more than one process?, Value: 2, ID: section-2
				78 text When does a part need more than one process?
			79 text A shaft with cross-holes, a turned fitting with milled flats or a housing with angled faces may need more than one operation or setup. The route should preserve the relationships between critical features. Send a 3D model and controlled 2D drawing so the team can review datums, access and inspection together.
			80 heading Is 5-axis machining always more accurate?, Value: 2, ID: section-3
				81 text Is 5-axis machining always more accurate?
			82 text No single process label guarantees accuracy. Acceptance depends on the part geometry, machine and setup, datum strategy, material, finishing and measurement method. If an interface matters, state its tolerance and datum on the drawing and ask how it will be produced and checked. Any tolerance target needs feature-specific review.
