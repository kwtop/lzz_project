						101 text How should I specify the material and finish?
					102 text Specify the exact material grade and condition, not just aluminum, steel or plastic. Identify cosmetic faces, surface treatment requirements, masking and dimensions that must be checked after finishing. The quotation should record the agreed specification.
				103 container engineering-3
					104 heading Can every feature hold ±0.01 mm?, Value: 3
						105 text Can every feature hold ±0.01 mm?
					106 text No. ±0.01 mm is a conditional engineering reference for selected critical features after review, not a general part tolerance or guarantee. Material, geometry, setup and measurement method determine whether it applies. Identify each critical feature on the drawing for confirmation.

For supplier qualification, request the current certificate for Hangzhou Lizhizao Precision Machinery Co., Ltd., including its number, issuer, validity, scope and verification details. Company awards are separate from the ISO certificate, and quality-system certification does not replace part-specific acceptance or industry approval.
